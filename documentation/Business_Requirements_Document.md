# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

# SkyLink Telecom
## Customer Intelligence & Predictive Analytics Platform

---

# 1. Project Overview

SkyLink Telecom is a fictional telecommunications company providing mobile, internet, and home phone services to residential customers across the United States.

The company has experienced an increase in customer churn, resulting in lost revenue, higher customer acquisition costs, and reduced customer lifetime value. Executive leadership has requested a predictive analytics solution capable of identifying customers who are at risk of leaving the company before they cancel their services.

This project simulates a real-world data science engagement by developing an end-to-end customer churn prediction platform. The solution combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, statistical analysis, machine learning, and business intelligence reporting to help leadership make proactive, data-driven customer retention decisions.

---

# 2. Company Background

SkyLink Telecom offers internet, phone, and bundled telecommunications services to residential customers nationwide.

Although the company continues to acquire new customers, retaining existing customers has become increasingly difficult due to growing market competition, contract flexibility, pricing, and changing customer expectations.

Currently, customer churn is analyzed only after customers have canceled their services. This reactive approach limits the organization's ability to intervene before revenue is lost.

To address this challenge, SkyLink Telecom requires a predictive analytics platform capable of identifying customers who are likely to churn, understanding the primary factors influencing customer attrition, and providing actionable recommendations that improve customer retention.

# 4. Business Problem

Customer churn has become one of the most significant business challenges facing SkyLink Telecom.

The company currently identifies churn only after customers have canceled their services, making it difficult to proactively retain valuable customers before revenue is lost.

Without an accurate method for identifying customers who are at risk of leaving, the company experiences increased customer acquisition costs, lower customer lifetime value, and reduced long-term profitability.

Executive leadership requires a predictive analytics solution capable of identifying high-risk customers before they churn, enabling proactive retention strategies that improve customer satisfaction and reduce revenue loss.

---

# 5. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Predict customers who are most likely to churn.
- Identify the primary factors contributing to customer churn.
- Develop an end-to-end machine learning solution for churn prediction.
- Compare multiple machine learning algorithms to determine the best-performing model.
- Provide data-driven recommendations that improve customer retention.
- Develop an executive Power BI dashboard for business decision making.

# 10. Assumptions

The project assumes:

- Historical customer data accurately represents customer behavior.
- The provided churn labels are correct.
- Customer behavior patterns within the dataset are representative of future customer behavior.
- Sufficient data quality exists for predictive modeling.
- Business stakeholders will use predictive insights to support retention strategies.

---

# 11. Risks & Constraints

Potential project risks include:

- Missing or inconsistent customer data.
- Class imbalance between churned and retained customers.
- Model overfitting.
- Dataset limitations due to simulated data.
- Business recommendations may require additional operational resources to implement.

---

# 12. Expected Business Value

Successful completion of this project will provide SkyLink Telecom with a predictive analytics platform capable of identifying customers who are likely to churn before they leave.

The resulting insights will help leadership prioritize customer retention efforts, improve marketing effectiveness, reduce customer acquisition costs, increase customer lifetime value, and support more informed business decisions through machine learning and interactive business intelligence reporting.
